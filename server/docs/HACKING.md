# Terran Hacking

The pinned `reference/cpp/trunk/database/Skills.xml` gives Hacking skill 26 to Terran Enforcers through rank 7 and Terran Scouts through rank 5. `reference/cpp/trunk/Net7/PlayerClass.cpp` maps IDs 57/58/76/16/13 to Hack Systems, Hack Weapons, Multi-Hack, Area System Hack, and Area Multi-Hack, and names native effect descriptor **193**. The executable `AHacking` handler is absent. The [Net-7 Hacking table](https://net7wiki.bmsite.net/index.php?title=Hacking) supplies selected MOB and area targeting, organic immunity, subsystem types, and range (1,000 plus 250 per trained rank). Archived `MOBClass.h` defines type 2/3 as organic red/green.

The server spends reactor energy at the native `0x58` ability request after an authoritative `0x17` hostile selection, emits existing charge and cooldown packets, and pins the selected MOB's death generation. Completion rechecks class/rank, life, visibility, hostility, range, sector and generation. Rank 6/7 plan hostile nonorganic MOBs within 1,500/3,000 of that selected center; other ranks affect only the selection. The world snapshot commits deterministic subsystem/weapon successes before the caster's completion receipt. Lost writes retry the same plan and never duplicate the MOB effect. Expired effects and changed death generations are rejected on reload. Successful active hacks publish descriptor 193 in native `0x09`, replay when the MOB becomes visible, and remove it with `0x0f` at expiry or visibility loss.

The MOB simulation currently has one weapon mount. Engine hacks stop chase, idle drift and fear displacement while permitting an in-range weapon. Reactor hacks block new weapon launches; there is no separate MOB reactor recharge loop to suppress. Weapon hacks block that mount's launches. Shield-system hacks leak half of shield-absorbed damage into hull until expiry. Multi-Hack's communications jam suppresses the existing native warning-hate routine; no separate assistance-call system exists. These effects are saved on the MOB and affect all pilots in the sector.

**Local tuning, not recovered native balance:** rank 1/3/5/6/7 costs 30/60/120/180/240 energy, charge lasts 2 seconds, cooldown 15 seconds, and successful effects last 8 seconds plus 2 seconds per trained rank. Each attempted system or weapon uses a deterministic roll with 50% base chance, +4 percentage points per trained rank, −2.5 points per MOB combat-level lead, plus the strongest equipped `STAT_HACKING` chance bonus. Chance is clamped to 5–95%. Catalog effects explicitly label that modifier as hacking success percentage; these exact success and duration formulas are local choices. The native client's appearance for descriptor 193 has not been visually verified in this checkpoint.

Disposable tests cover rank/class and organic rejection; shared two-pilot area targeting; a failed world write followed by retry and restart; a killed selected generation; engine, reactor, shield and weapon consequences; equipped hacking chance; and `0x09` replay/`0x0f` expiry. No live process, account, or client was used.
