# Self Destruct source audit and implementation

Pinned `reference/cpp/trunk/database/Skills.xml` grants activated skill 50 to **Progen Warrior only**, as a quest skill learned at overall level 40. `PlayerSkills.h` assigns ability IDs 100–104, and `PlayerClass.cpp` maps them to `ASelfDestruct` at ranks 1/3/5/6/7 and explicitly names native visual descriptor **206**. The executable handler is missing from the pinned tree. The server catalog now removes the erroneous Jenquai Seeker entry and offers the five Warrior ranks.

The [Net-7 Self Destruct table](https://net7wiki.bmsite.net/index.php?title=Self_Destruct) gives Absolute blast damage per trained skill rank of 126/252/810/2520/6000 for the five ability bands. Radius starts at 1,000 and grows by 250 per trained rank to 2,500. It states that the user's reactor energy and shields drop to zero, and the ship becomes temporarily unable to act while enemies in radius take damage. The [original player guide](https://www.earthnbeyond.com/game_info/skills.progen_w.html) confirms the Warrior blast harms surrounding enemies and grows stronger with rank. Neither source gives an exact charge, recovery, or cooldown time.

The public catalog has `SKILL_SELF_DESTRUCT` equipped values from +30 to +118 on Dagger, Scimitar, and Plague items. The [Plague Octo item page](https://net7wiki.bmsite.net/index.php?title=Plague_Octo) describes a variable damage bonus, but the archived executable formula is absent. The server treats these values as a **local percentage damage bonus** capped at 200%. It freezes the bonus in the pending cast and sector receipt, without changing trained rank or radius.

`self_destruct.rs` builds a duplicate-free, generation-pinned blast plan for hostile live MOBs in radius. The session checkpoints a pending cast, and the sector persists damage plus a receipt in one world snapshot. A subsequent session checkpoint drains reactor and shield and records a temporary action lock. After a crash between those writes, replay reads the receipt without damaging the same life again. A failed sector write rolls the damage back. The blast has native visual descriptor 206, and the temporary lock does not set hull to zero or invoke death/tow behavior. Friendly pilots are excluded.

The sources do not give a charge, cooldown, or lock duration. The local catalog uses a 3 second charge, 60 second cooldown, and 10 second action lock; these are tuning choices. Tests use disposable worlds and do not run or restart the live server or client.
