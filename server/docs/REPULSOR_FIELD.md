# Repulsor Field authoritative implementation

Repulsor Field is the Progen Sentinel's activated skill 48 in the pinned `reference/cpp/trunk/database/Skills.xml`. `reference/cpp/trunk/Net7/PlayerClass.cpp` maps Minor/Lesser/Repulsor/Greater/Major to ability IDs 75/70/96/50/72 at ranks 1/3/5/6/7. The tree does **not** contain the executable `ARepulsorField` handler.

The [Net-7 Repulsor Field table](https://net7wiki.bmsite.net/index.php?title=Repulsor_Field) supplies the authoritative source for Energy feedback: 20/40/60/60/60% of post-resist incoming damage, capped at 15/30/60/120/240 damage per effective skill level, with reactor costs 50/125/250/500/1000. The affected incoming types are Impact, Explosive, Plasma, Energy, EMP, and Chemical. Rank 1 is self-only. Rank 3 and above permit a selected friendly pilot; no group membership requirement is listed. Target range is 2,500 at rank 1 and grows by 250 per trained rank through 4,000 at rank 7. Intermediate trained ranks improve range and the feedback cap without unlocking the next ability ID.

The public item catalog has four `SKILL_REPULSOR_FIELD` equipped effects: item 2420 (+4.33), 5338 (+3.33), 5373 (+2.96), and 5374 (+2.59). `repulsor_field.rs` accepts the frozen additive gear bonus for the cap and duration but leaves the trained-rank ability gate unchanged. Effective rank is bounded at 20 to avoid unbounded stacked catalog effects.

The module prepares a recipient-epoch/revision pinned buff and deterministic feedback plan. In central integration, compute feedback from `DamageResult.reported_damage` (post-deflect), commit its value in the existing NPC-shot receipt together with HP, and hand the same frozen value to the combat world's one-shot `acknowledge_mob_hit`. The existing receipt validation allows at most 50 reflection and must be raised to the bounded Repulsor maximum while rejecting forged or nonfinite values. Reflected damage is Energy and is applied to the frozen attacker life only. A cast should commit caster energy and recipient status together in the world ledger, then replay without charging again after a torn recipient checkpoint.

**Local tuning:** two-second charge, 30-second cooldown, and duration of 60 seconds plus 15 seconds per additional effective skill level. The public tier description says duration increases but gives no exact numbers. No Repulsor visual descriptor is verified in the archived tree, so this implementation sends a native timed ship AUX status and message without a guessed 0x09 visual. The legacy client's exact displayed icon/text still needs live visual confirmation before claiming parity.

The native ship AUX contains sixteen buff slots. Combat Trance occupies slot 9 only for Warriors; Power Down occupies slot 12 only for Sentinels. Repulsor uses slot 12 on Warrior recipients and slot 9 on all other recipient classes, preserving both simultaneous combinations. The caster's charged ability and the recipient's frozen field are checkpointed through the same durable energy ledger. NPC reflection is calculated from `DamageResult.reported_damage` after deflection and stored in the same durable hit receipt as HP; a repeated world acknowledgement reuses that value rather than damaging the MOB twice. Reflection is Energy type, applies only to the frozen attacker, and does not create a reciprocal reflection loop.

The two-client test covers selected range, a torn recipient checkpoint, server restart, exactly-once caster energy, and native status replay. Other focused tests cover Power Down/Combat Trance slot coexistence, expiry, and post-deflect NPC hit replay. No live server or client was used.
