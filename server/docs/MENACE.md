# Menace evidence and local rules

The [official Earth & Beyond manual](https://downloads.cncfps.com/Westwood/earthandbeyond/manual/EBMANUAL.PDF) describes Menace as frightening enemies into flight, with area effects at higher skill levels. The [Net-7 Menace table](https://net7wiki.bmsite.net/index.php?title=Menace) identifies Intimidate, Scare, Terrify, Area Intimidation and Area Terrify, with increasing target range, 1,500/3,000-unit area radii, and higher-tier weapon and special-ability suppression. The archived `PlayerSkills.h` assigns ability IDs 64, 99, 127, 11 and 18. `PlayerClass.cpp` names `AMenace`, but its executable handler is missing from the pinned reference; its comment identifies visual effect 198 for Intimidate and 199 for Scare only.

The `menace.rs` rules and shared MOB combat path enforce target revalidation after the charged cast, range and area admission, a deterministic resistance roll, and durable fear pinned to the MOB death generation. Frightened MOBs fly away from the caster's cast position. Intimidate and Area Intimidation still permit weapon fire during flight. Scare suppresses special abilities; Terrify and Area Terrify suppress both weapons and special abilities. Area casts use the selected hostile MOB as their center and exclude friendly MOBs. The world checkpoint commits all affected MOBs together, so a failed save retries without rerolling or double-charging. Intimidate and Scare use the two archived native visual IDs; observer sessions publish their 0x09 effects after spawn and 0x0f removal at expiry, with replay after replacement.

**Local tuning, not recovered balance:** the rules use a 1.5-second cast, 15-second cooldown, energy costs 25/45/65/90/120, an 8-second base duration plus 2 seconds per trained rank, 150 m/s flight speed, and a level-sensitive 5–90% success chance. Higher-level targets resist more often. The rank-one range of 2,500 is inferred from the documented +250 increments beginning with rank two. No unverified Terrify or area visual descriptor is assigned. Existing MOB AI has no special-ability launches yet, so Scare's ability suppression becomes observable when that AI feature exists.
