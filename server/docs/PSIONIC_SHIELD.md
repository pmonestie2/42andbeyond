# Psionic Shield evidence and authoritative behavior

The [official Earth & Beyond manual](https://downloads.cncfps.com/Westwood/earthandbeyond/manual/EBMANUAL.PDF) says the Defender's shield protects another pilot until it absorbs a fixed amount of damage. The [Net-7 Psionic Shield table](https://net7wiki.bmsite.net/index.php?title=Psionic_Shield) specifies +30 psionic resistance, absorption per trained skill level of 30/90/360/720/1440 for the five abilities, and selected-player range increasing from 2,500 to 4,000. `reference/cpp/trunk/Net7/PlayerSkills.h` assigns IDs 79/69/81/49/80. `PlayerClass.cpp` explicitly labels effect 214 for Psionic Shield, but the pinned tree has no executable `APsionicShield` handler.

The `psionic_shield.rs` module encodes all five tiers, a recipient-epoch and world-revision pinned absorption balance, a pure one-hit interception plan, and selected-player range validation. The balance is **not** ordinary ship shield points: it intercepts incoming NPC damage before typed deflect and normal shield/hull damage, then dissipates at zero or its deadline. The +30 psionic resistance applies to the remaining psionic damage. On restart, a recipient checkpoint at the same or newer revision retains its spent balance instead of being refilled from the original world grant. An NPC shot receipt commits the barrier's new balance together with ship HP, so retry cannot spend it twice.

**Local tuning:** five-minute maximum lifetime, two-second charge, 30-second cooldown, and reactor costs 50/100/150/225/300. The public material does not give these values. The native visual descriptor 214 is source-backed. Activation/replay publish native ship AUX buff slot 14 (`Psionic_Shield`) and 0x09 ObjectEffect; depletion, expiry and replacement remove the old visual with 0x0f and clear the slot. The recipient receives authoritative text with the current absorption balance. The cast revalidates visible recipient, alive/space state, sector and range at completion. The world ledger commits caster energy and recipient grant together, while the recipient checkpoint carries the changing balance and each NPC-hit receipt. Existing clients can use the native status/effect packets; no client deployment or live visual confirmation is claimed.
