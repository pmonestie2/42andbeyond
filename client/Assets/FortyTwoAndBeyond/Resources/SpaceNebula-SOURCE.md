# Space nebula panorama

`SpaceNebula.png` was generated for this project with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-24. It has no external image source. The generated bytes were copied into `Resources` without editing.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept  
> Asset type: panoramic sky texture for a 3D space game, sampled as an equirectangular environment behind gameplay  
> Primary request: cinematic deep-space star field with a restrained belt of layered interstellar dust and gas. Rich physical texture and depth, modern science-fiction game rendering quality. Deep navy and near-black with muted indigo, faint teal, and small warm amber pockets. Sparse stars of varied brightness, a few subtle dust lanes.  
> Composition/framing: panoramic wide view with the most detailed nebula structure around the middle horizontal third. Keep the central 25 percent fairly dark so ships and target labels stay readable. Left and right edges should join as closely as possible when tiled horizontally. No big bright focal planet or large object.  
> Lighting/mood: low-key, cinematic, realistic, deep contrast.  
> Constraints: background only; no ships, spacecraft, planets, UI, text, logos, symbols, borders, watermark, or large white streaks. The image should remain subtle behind foreground actors.

The `SpaceSky` shader uses this image only when the generated
`ProceduralSpace.png` panorama is absent. The primary panorama is created by
`client/Tools/generate_space_sky.py` and includes its stars and nebula offline.
