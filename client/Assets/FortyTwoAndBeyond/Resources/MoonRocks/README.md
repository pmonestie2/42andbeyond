# Moon rock models

`moon_rock_01` and `moon_rock_03` come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) and are [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Original asset pages: [Moon Rock 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_01) and [Moon Rock 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_03). Photography: Greg Zaal and Rico Cilliers; processing: Jenelle van Heerden, plus Dario Barresi for Moon Rock 01.

The 1K diffuse, normal, and AO/roughness/metal maps are copied unchanged from Poly Haven's model download. The OBJ meshes were exported from the source glTF into centered game coordinates. Moon Rock 01 uses the source's highest and lowest supplied meshes (9,958 and 1,244 triangles). Moon Rock 03 uses its 5,598-triangle source mesh and a 1,100-triangle simplified LOD. Materials and mineral accents are authored in Unity for a stylized space setting.

Source glTF SHA-256: `moon_rock_01_1k.gltf` `004cc353fa0c29572a9ba40535f3716c297466fdf2c650d36deaf9e27eed5ce7`; `moon_rock_03_1k.gltf` `aee7860497473cba4d06038abaa37e6da675cc97a4e7fa2fdee28ebf8844eea1`.
